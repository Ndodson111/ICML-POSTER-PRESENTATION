Per-noise level memorization

What we measure
g = mθσ(xtest + σZ).

We call this one-step output memorized when it lands clearly closer to one training image than to the runner-up:

d1NN(g) < d2NN(g) 3 .

This is the paper’s exact per-noise criterion.

  • A trained EDM on 1K images only sees per-noise level memorization in intermediate noise levels.
